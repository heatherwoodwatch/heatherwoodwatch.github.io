Privacy Policy

Heatherwood Watch – neighborhood safety text alerts · Heatherwood neighborhood, Apopka, FL
Last updated: November 17, 2026

Heatherwood Watch ("we," "us") is a volunteer-run text message service that sends safety alerts and occasional community notices to residents of the Heatherwood neighborhood who have signed up. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Sign-up information: your name, street address in the neighborhood, and mobile phone number, provided on our paper or web sign-up form.
  • Web sign-up form: if you sign up online, we also keep which alerts you chose (text messages, Discord, and/or "tell me about WhatsApp"), whether you also want HOA meeting notices or community event messages, whether you ticked the box saying you might be interested in helping as a neighborhood captain someday, the consent wording shown to you, the date and time, the browser type reported by your device, and a shortened form of your internet address (the last part removed), as our record of your consent. If you give an email address, we keep it with your member record so Heatherwood Watch can contact you about the service (for example, to send your Discord invite or tell you about WhatsApp). We don't use it for anything else. If you choose Discord only, we never text your number; it's used only to identify you. Sign-ups that aren't approved are kept as a record but never receive texts.
  • Spam protection: the web form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to block automated sign-ups. Cloudflare processes your browser and connection information for that check under its own privacy policy.
  • Member list: for each enrolled resident we keep your name, street address, mobile number, email address (if you gave one), which channels you use, and which optional messages you've chosen, so the neighborhood captains can confirm residents and manage the list.
  • Messages you send us: the text and any photos you send to the Heatherwood Watch number, such as reports of suspicious activity.
  • Basic message records: dates, times, and delivery status of messages sent to and from your number.

How we use your information

  • To send you neighborhood safety alerts, and, only if you choose them, HOA meeting notices and community event messages.
  • To let enrolled residents report activity, and to let our volunteer neighborhood captains review those reports before an alert is sent.
  • To confirm your enrollment, respond to HELP requests, and honor STOP (opt-out) requests.
  • To keep a record of your consent to receive messages.
We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. We share information only with the service providers needed to deliver the messages (our SMS provider, Twilio, and Discord if you choose Discord alerts) or when required by law. Reports of suspicious activity or crime, with related photos and notes, may be shared with law enforcement (such as the Orange County Sheriff's Office) to help them investigate; the reporter's name and phone number are included only when needed for that purpose. A report you submit may be included in an alert sent to enrolled residents after review by a neighborhood captain; captains may edit reports to remove unnecessary personal details before sending.

How we protect and keep your information

Information is stored on a private, password-protected system run by a resident volunteer and is accessible only to Heatherwood Watch neighborhood captains. Signed paper forms and web sign-up records are kept on file as records of consent. If you opt out, we stop messaging you and remove your number from the active alert list; we may keep a record of your opt-out so we don't message you again.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages.
  • Reply HOA OFF or EVENTS OFF to stop HOA meeting notices or community event messages while still getting safety alerts (HOA ON / EVENTS ON to start them).
  • Reply HELP for help.
  • Contact us to ask what information we hold about you or to have it deleted.
