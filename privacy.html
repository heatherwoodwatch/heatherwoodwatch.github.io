Privacy Policy

Heatherwood Watch – neighborhood safety text alerts · Heatherwood neighborhood, Apopka, FL
Last updated: November 16, 2026

Heatherwood Watch ("we," "us") is a volunteer-run text message service that sends safety alerts and occasional community notices to residents of the Heatherwood neighborhood who have signed up. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Sign-up information: your name, street address in the neighborhood, and mobile phone number, provided on our paper or web sign-up form.
  • Web sign-up form: if you sign up online, we also keep which alerts you chose (text messages, Discord, and/or "tell me about WhatsApp"), the consent wording shown to you, the date and time, the browser type reported by your device, and a shortened form of your internet address (the last part removed), as our record of your consent. If you give an email address, we use it only to send your Discord invite or tell you about WhatsApp. If you choose Discord only, we never text your number; it's used only to identify you. Sign-ups that aren't approved are kept as a record but never receive texts.
  • Spam protection: the web form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to block automated sign-ups. Cloudflare processes your browser and connection information for that check under its own privacy policy.
  • Messages you send us: the text and any photos you send to the Heatherwood Watch number, such as reports of suspicious activity.
  • Basic message records: dates, times, and delivery status of messages sent to and from your number.

How we use your information

  • To send you neighborhood safety alerts and community notices you signed up for.
  • To let enrolled residents report activity, and to let our volunteer admins review those reports before an alert is sent.
  • To confirm your enrollment, respond to HELP requests, and honor STOP (opt-out) requests.
  • To keep a record of your consent to receive messages.
We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. We share information only with the service providers needed to deliver the messages (our SMS provider, Twilio) or when required by law. A report you submit may be included in an alert sent to enrolled residents after admin review; admins may edit reports to remove unnecessary personal details before sending.

How we protect and keep your information

Information is stored on a private, password-protected system run by a resident volunteer and is accessible only to Heatherwood Watch admins. Signed paper forms and web sign-up records are kept on file as records of consent. If you opt out, we stop messaging you and remove your number from the active alert list; we may keep a record of your opt-out so we don't message you again.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages.
  • Reply HELP for help.
  • Contact us to ask what information we hold about you or to have it deleted.
