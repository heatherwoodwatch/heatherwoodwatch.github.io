Heatherwood Watch

A volunteer-run neighborhood safety text alert program for residents of the Heatherwood neighborhood in Apopka, FL. Enrolled residents can text reports to (689) 210-2210; volunteer admins review each report before any alert is sent to enrolled residents.

This is not 911. If anyone is in danger or a crime is in progress, call 911 first.
