Heatherwood Watch – neighborhood safety text alerts · Heatherwood neighborhood, Apopka, FL
Last updated: November 16, 2026

Heatherwood Watch is a free, volunteer-run text message program for residents of the Heatherwood neighborhood in Apopka, Florida. By signing up, you agree to these terms.

SMS Terms

  • Program name: Heatherwood Watch.
  • What you'll receive: neighborhood safety alerts approved by volunteer admins, occasional community notices, confirmations when you submit a report, and replies to HELP/STOP requests. Enrolled residents may also text the number to report suspicious or criminal activity.
  • How to sign up: complete and sign the Heatherwood Watch paper sign-up form, or fill in the web sign-up form at https://watch.yourfavoritefriend.com/join and tick the text-message consent box. A volunteer neighborhood captain checks every sign-up against the neighborhood before adding you, and you will receive a confirmation text once enrolled. (Choosing only Discord on the web form means you won't receive texts.)
  • Message frequency varies based on neighborhood activity.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • To opt out, reply STOP at any time. You'll receive one final message confirming you've been unsubscribed. To rejoin, sign up again.
  • For help, reply HELP or email heatherwoodwatchapk@gmail.com.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  • Consent to receive messages is not a condition of any purchase or service.

Not an emergency service

Heatherwood Watch is not a substitute for 911. Alerts may be delayed or not delivered, and every report is reviewed by volunteers before it is shared. If anyone is in danger or a crime is in progress, call 911 first. Do not confront anyone; observe from a safe place and contact law enforcement.

Using the program responsibly

  • Report only what you have actually observed, as accurately as you can. Describe what someone is doing, plus clothing and vehicle details, rather than assumptions.
  • Do not send false, harassing, or abusive reports. Admins may decline, edit, or not send any report, and may remove participants who misuse the program.
  • Photos you send may be shared with enrolled residents if approved by admins.
