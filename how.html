Reports and photos

Same as texting: just write what you see, photos welcome. NUMBERS and HELP work here too.

What makes a good report

Captains have to decide quickly whether to alert everyone. Give them what, where, which way, and what it looks like.

Hard to act on

Send photos and short videos right in the text

  • Attach them the way you would to family. No app, no upload page. Discord works too.
  • Everything is saved with your report. Captains see it, and it goes into the case file if the sheriff needs it.
  • Photos go out with the alert, with the hidden location data removed. Videos stay with the captains and the case file.
  • Sent a few things in a row? Texts, photos and videos sent within a few minutes of each other become one report.

After an alert, check your cameras

One person sees a moment. A street full of doorbell and driveway cameras sees the whole route. When an alert goes out, look at your footage around that time and text what you find. Captains put it all together.

What to send back

The time on your footage, which way the person or vehicle was going, and a photo or short clip. A screenshot of your camera app is fine.

Nothing on camera?

That helps too. "Nothing on my driveway cam 9:30 to 9:50" tells captains which way they didn't go.

Leave the detective work to the sheriff

Don't post plates, names or guesses in group chats or social media. Send it here and captains pass it on.

Good to know

This is not 911

If anyone is in danger or a crime is happening, call 911 first. Then let the neighborhood know.

Don't confront anyone

Watch from somewhere safe. A photo is only worth it if you can take it without being noticed.

Your name stays private

Captains see who sent a report. The neighborhood alert doesn't include your name.

Photos are cleaned

Before a photo goes out, the hidden location data your phone adds is removed.

Real people check every report

Neighborhood captains are volunteers who live here. Nothing goes out to everyone until they approve it.

Neighbors only

Only residents a captain has checked can report or get alerts. Texts from unknown numbers are ignored.

Questions

Short answers to what neighbors ask most.

How many texts will I get?

Only when something happens that captains think everyone should know, plus your confirmation text. HOA and event notices only come if you turn them on.

Does it cost anything?

No. It's free and run by volunteers. Your carrier's normal message and data rates may apply.

Can everyone in my house sign up?

Yes. Each adult signs up with their own mobile number, so alerts reach each of you.

Who sees my phone number?

Only the neighborhood captains, to know who sent a report. It's never shared or sold.

What if I send something by mistake?

No harm done. Captains read every report first. They can just file it without alerting anyone.

I'd rather not get texts. Can I still be in the loop?

Yes. Choose Discord only when you sign up. Discord is a free app for your phone or computer, and alerts also post in its #alerts channel.
